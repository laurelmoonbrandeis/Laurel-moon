    text: |-
      You’re a virgin of
      sacrifice. A goddess
      feeding the harvest moon
      palm frond offerings
      from your palms.
      The remaining soil
      in your fist will turn
      the full sun into nectar
      under Mercury’s core,
      an earth apple spotted
      with gold-dusted chamomile.
      Bathe in the lavender dew

      of mercurial sea until rose-
      mary needles of golden rain,

      an Aphroditic ear of grain,
      drapes around your head.
      Tie the knot connecting
      galaxy clusters to Spica,
      spiked star shimmering with
      vestal Vindemiatrix, divine
      grape gatherer. Then cultivate
      Mars globes of lavandula.

      Marriage to the jaded king
      of herbs is inevitable. Sacred
      leaves always seep through
      Mars’ aquatic craters. Basil
      particularly sprinkles out of
      nowhere: from holy water, tulsi,
      a potted pom-pom poem.
      Sometimes from a Satanic
      symbol, a peppermint omen.
      Remember to place it in
      your hands. Under the mouth.
      Surrender. Pray to reach
      a blue god, maybe Vishnu.
      Then settle, with the atmosphere
      melting away in ablation.
